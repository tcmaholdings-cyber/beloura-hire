How the marks are used

  • Live chats is the overall grade (mean of the judged chats). At or above the pass mark passes; between the review floor and the pass mark is a review; below the floor fails.
  • Chat fluency is the judge's casual-chat-English sub-score (0-5), averaged across the chats. It grades texting register, not formal English: slang and lowercase are fine; incoherent or stiff formal writing is marked down.
  • Typing WPM is computed from the real typed characters in the chat replies (pasted messages are left out).
  • Download speed comes from a silent probe that runs on the chat page; if it did not run, the speed check is skipped, not failed.
  • AI-use risk above the mark sends the result to review for a person to check. It is never an automatic fail on its own.
  • Number of fan chats and the per-message clock / idle gap control the length of the test.
